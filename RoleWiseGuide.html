Super Admin

Platform-wide administrator with full access across all schools and modules
Level 1 — Highest Authority
Access Summary Super Admin has unrestricted access to the entire platform. Can manage multiple schools, view cross-school analytics, configure system settings, and override any school-level configuration. This role operates at the platform level, not school level.
✔ Full Platform Access ✔ All Schools ✔ System Configuration ✔ Subscription Management ✔ Audit Logs

Modules & Features

📊 Platform Overview
  • Platform-wide dashboard & KPIs
  • Total schools, users, revenue stats
  • System health monitoring
  • Recent activity feed
🏫 Schools Management
  • Create & onboard new schools
  • Edit school profiles & settings
  • View school-wise reports
  • Activate / deactivate schools
💳 Subscription & Billing
  • Manage subscription plans
  • View payments & invoices
  • Revenue analytics dashboard
  • Upgrade / downgrade plans
👥 Users Management
  • Manage admins, teachers, staff
  • Manage students, parents, accountants
  • Manage librarians & transport users
  • Bulk user operations
📚 Academics (Global)
  • Classes, sections, subjects
  • Academic years management
  • Boards, board classes
  • Chapters & topics
📈 Reports & Analytics
  • School-wise performance reports
  • Finance & academic analytics
  • Platform usage statistics
  • Activity logs & audit trails
⚙️ Master Configurations
  • Fee categories management
  • Departments & designations
  • Global system configuration
  • Platform-wide settings
🔧 System Control
  • Roles & permissions management
  • Module enable/disable
  • System backup & restore
  • Attendance & full audit logs
